# Decision: copyright stays with Avanade Inc.

Date: 2026-11-05
Decided by: Steve Marks

Kryptonite's K-03 flagged that `LICENSE` says "Copyright (c) 2026 Avanade Inc." on a repo under Steve's personal GitHub account. The options were: switch to personal copyright, keep Avanade, or make the repo private until it's cleared.

**Decision:** keep Avanade Inc. as the copyright holder (MIT). This is consistent with Steve's other published skills. Steve owns clearing it with Avanade's open-source process.

**Risk accepted:** until that clearance exists, the attribution is Steve's call as an Avanade Principal. If Avanade asks, the fallback is to transfer the repo to an Avanade org or make it private.
